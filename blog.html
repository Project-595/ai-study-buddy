STUDY WITH AI

USE AI AS
YOUR STUDY PARTNER.

AI can be useful when you use it to understand concepts, practice questions and explore topics rather than simply asking it to complete your academic work. Explore our AI study tools for students to find tools that can support your learning.

🧠

Ask for Explanations

When a topic is difficult, ask AI to explain the concept in simpler language and provide examples.

❓

Practice Questions

Ask AI to create questions about a topic and test yourself before checking the answers.

🔁

Learn Through Revision

Use AI to turn your notes into summaries, flashcards or revision questions.

PROMPT BETTER

ASK BETTER.
GET BETTER.

The quality of an AI response often depends on how clearly you describe what you need. Specific prompts can make AI responses more useful for studying.

🎯

Be Specific

Clearly explain the subject, topic and type of help you need instead of asking a vague question.

🎓

Give Context

Tell the AI your learning level, assignment topic or specific difficulty so the response is more relevant.

🔍

Ask Follow-Up Questions

If the explanation is unclear, ask AI to simplify, provide another example or explain a different way.

LEARN ACTIVELY

DON'T JUST
READ. THINK.

Learning becomes stronger when you actively recall, question and apply what you have learned.

📝

Test Yourself

Try answering questions without looking at your notes before checking the correct answer.

💭

Explain It Yourself

Explain a concept in your own words to check whether you actually understand it.

🛠️

Apply What You Learn

Use concepts in examples, exercises or projects instead of relying only on memorization.

USE RESPONSIBLY

AI HELPS YOU.
YOU DO THE THINKING.

AI should support your learning rather than replace your own thinking, research and academic effort.

🧩

Understand Before Using

Make sure you understand AI-generated answers before including ideas in your academic work.

✅

Check Important Information

Verify important facts using reliable sources because AI responses can sometimes be incorrect.

📚

Follow Academic Rules

Check your school or university's rules about using AI for assignments, assessments and research.

EXAM PREP

GET READY
FOR EXAMS.

AI can help organize revision and create practice material, but effective exam preparation still requires consistent study and active practice.

📅

Create a Study Plan

Break subjects into smaller topics and create a realistic revision schedule.

🎯

Focus on Weak Areas

Use practice questions to identify topics that need more attention.

🔥

Practice Consistently

Short, regular study sessions can be more useful than leaving all revision until the last minute.

THE AI STUDY RULE

USE AI
TO GET BETTER.

Let AI help you understand, practice, explore and create. But keep your curiosity, judgment and original thinking at the center of your education.
