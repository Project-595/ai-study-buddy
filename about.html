About AI Study Buddy

AI Study Buddy is a student-focused resource designed to help students discover useful AI tools for their academic and study needs.

Our Purpose

AI Study Buddy makes it easier for students to explore AI tools for studying, writing, research, presentations, coding and productivity.

What We Cover

📚 Study

Tools and resources that can support learning, revision and exam preparation.

✍️ Writing

AI tools that can assist with writing, grammar, summaries and academic communication.

🔬 Research

Resources for finding information and understanding academic research.

🎨 Presentations

Tools that can help students plan and create engaging presentations.

💻 Coding

AI resources that can support programming education and coding practice.

🎯 Productivity

AI tools and study tips that can help students organize their academic workflow.

Our Approach

AI Study Buddy encourages students to use artificial intelligence as a learning assistant. Students should always verify important information and use AI responsibly.
