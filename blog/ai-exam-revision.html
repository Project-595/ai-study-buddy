HOW TO USE AI FOR EXAM REVISION.

Use AI to organise your revision, practise recalling information and identify topics that need more attention. The goal is to learn the material, not simply generate more notes.

1. Start with your syllabus

Before asking AI to create a revision plan, list your subjects, exam dates and topics. Include the time you can realistically study each day.

AI can help turn this information into a timetable. Check that the plan covers your actual syllabus and leaves time for practice and rest.

Try this prompt

Create a 7-day revision plan for my upcoming exam. My subject is [subject], my exam date is [date], and the topics I need to study are [topic list]. I can study for [time] each day. Prioritise difficult topics and include practice questions and review sessions.

2. Turn your notes into practice questions

Practice questions help you discover what you remember without looking at your notes. Ask AI to generate short answer questions, multiple-choice questions or questions that require you to explain a concept.

Try this prompt

Using only the notes below, create 10 exam-style questions. Do not show the answers until I have attempted every question. After I respond, mark my answers and explain any mistakes. Notes: [paste your notes]

Check generated questions against your syllabus and your teacher's instructions. AI-generated questions may not match the format or difficulty of your actual exam.

3. Use active recall instead of rereading

Active recall means trying to retrieve information from memory before checking the answer. Ask AI to quiz you one question at a time, then explain what you missed.

For a stronger learning routine, answer from memory first, check the explanation, and try again later. You can also turn important definitions, processes and formulas into flashcards.

Explore our guide on making AI flashcards and practice quizzes .

4. Ask AI to explain difficult topics

If a concept is confusing, ask for a simpler explanation, a worked example and then a question for you to solve independently. This can make difficult material easier to approach without replacing your textbook or course notes.

Try this prompt

Explain [topic] in simple language for a student at [level]. Give one worked example, point out common misunderstandings, and then give me a new problem to solve without showing the solution first.

5. Track weak areas

Keep a list of questions you answered incorrectly and topics you could not explain clearly. Ask AI to group the mistakes by topic and suggest what to practise next.

Do not let AI decide that you have mastered a topic just because you understand its explanation. Test yourself without assistance to check what you can actually recall.

6. Check facts and protect your information

AI can produce incorrect dates, formulas, definitions or explanations. Verify important information using your textbook, class notes, trusted educational sources or your teacher.

Avoid uploading private information, student records or confidential assessment material. Follow your institution's rules on AI use.

A simple AI revision routine

  1. Choose one topic from your syllabus.
  2. Review your notes and identify what you do not understand.
  3. Ask AI to explain the difficult parts.
  4. Answer practice questions without looking at the answers.
  5. Check your mistakes against reliable sources.
  6. Schedule another attempt at the questions you missed.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI replace studying for exams?

No. AI can help you practise and understand topics, but you still need to learn the material, follow your syllabus and test your own knowledge.

Can AI make an exam revision timetable?

Yes. Provide your exam dates, topics, available study time and priorities. Review the timetable to make sure it is realistic and complete.

Should I trust every answer AI gives me?

No. Verify important claims, calculations and explanations with reliable sources.

Make your revision more effective

Use AI to practise, find gaps in your understanding and organise your time. Keep your syllabus and reliable learning materials at the centre of your revision.
